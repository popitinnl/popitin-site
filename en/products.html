Two machines, one platform

One platform, two form factors. Choose NOMAD when you need to move fast — new locations, seasonal footfall, events. Choose DOCK when you need a permanent, high-throughput return point.

NOMAD · mobile

Move fast, stand anywhere.

Battery-powered (900 Wh, up to 24 hours or ±1000 containers) and on wheels. Operational within fifteen minutes at a festival, pop-up or seasonal location — no power socket required.

The stayer for busy locations.
